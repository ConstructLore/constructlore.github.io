What it is

From Noise to Intelligence.

Data center teams generate thousands of records across commissioning platforms, schedules, issue logs, meeting minutes, vendor trackers, and field reports. CLIP connects those records and identifies the actions, risks, and decisions that matter most, before milestones are impacted.

CLIP is not a dashboard, and not another tool to feed. It is the intelligence platform for data center delivery: it reads the issue logs, checklists, and commissioning records your project already generates and returns the answers that decide energization dates. What blocks power-up. Which issues matter most. Which vendors are at risk. Which assets are ready. Where schedule risk is forming.

Nothing to adopt, nothing new to feed. An intelligence layer over the work you are already doing, rendered as a single file that opens on a boardroom laptop, in a site trailer, or fully offline. Every number labeled, sourced, and recomputable.
